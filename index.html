How I work

From Business Challenge to Working Solution

A disciplined process keeps the work anchored to the business need from discovery through delivery.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Understand the business problem, existing workflow, data sources, users and expected outcome.

  2. 02

    Assess

    Identify gaps, bottlenecks, manual work, data quality issues and technical constraints.

  3. 03

    Design

    Design a practical solution architecture appropriate to the requirement.

  4. 04

    Build

    Implement the required data, cloud, analytics, automation or AI components.

  5. 05

    Validate

  6. 06

    Deliver
